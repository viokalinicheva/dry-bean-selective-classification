NEW DERIVED WORK
Violetta Kalinicheva, Dry Bean Classification, November 2026. New analysis products
include exact-vector grouping, partition metadata, fitted models, prediction
caches, metrics, bootstrap draws, evaluation-weight sensitivity, figures, and
written interpretation. No source rows/features were deleted, relabeled,
imputed, joined, or unit-converted. Fold-local StandardScaler transformation is
used for logistic regression only. These changes do not imply endorsement by
the data creators, UCI, or cited authors.
